# Upsi: an alarm for your Mac with a photo on a wrong password

Upsi is an alarm you install on your own Mac. When someone wakes your locked Mac (opens the lid or presses a key) or types a wrong password, Upsi takes a photo with the Mac's camera. The photo stays on your Mac. If you want, Upsi also sends it to you on Telegram, with the time.

## How it works

1. **Install the app.** Drag it into the Applications folder and open it. macOS asks for permission to use the camera, and the Upsi eye appears in the menu bar.
2. **Connect it to Telegram (optional).** If you want the alerts on your phone, you make your own bot in Telegram and Upsi sends the alerts there. The app shows every step, with pictures.
3. **Go about your day.** Lock the screen and leave. If someone wakes your locked Mac, you get the alert.

## What you get

- **The photo**, taken the moment someone woke the locked screen or typed a wrong password. You choose how many photos Upsi takes per alert.
- **The time**, always. **The place** only if you turned location on, with a link to a map.
- **USB stick (beta).** If someone plugs in a USB stick while the Mac is locked, Upsi takes a photo then too.
- **Shoulder Watch (beta).** When you work in a café or on a train, the eye in the menu bar turns red if someone behind you looks at your screen. If you want, the screen blurs until the person leaves. Upsi does not save or send anything the camera sees for this.
- **Mirror mode.** A small window with the camera picture, like a mirror, so you see who is behind you without turning around.

## Visible by design

The camera light turns on at every photo. The Upsi eye stays in the menu bar and the app has no option to hide it. At the first unlock after installing, and then at most once every 7 days, the message "Upsi runs on this Mac" appears, and it cannot be turned off.

Upsi is meant only for a Mac that belongs to you. The Terms forbid using it to watch another person. If you think someone installed Upsi without your consent on a computer you use, write to contact@getupsi.com.

## Your data

- **Where are the photos?** On your Mac, in Pictures › Upsi. You can choose another folder in the settings. They delete themselves after the number of days you choose (30 by default).
- **Who else sees them?** Only you. If you connected your Telegram bot, the photos also go to the chat with your own bot. They pass through Telegram's servers like any Telegram message (chats with bots are not end-to-end encrypted), but never through ours. We have no server that receives them.
- **What do we receive?** Only the licence key, when Upsi checks that it is valid, and the question of whether a new version is out.
